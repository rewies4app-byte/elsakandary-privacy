٥النسخة الاحتياطية

  • يُحفَظ في المكان الذي تختاره عبر منتقي ملفات النظام

بعد أن يغادر الملف التطبيق تصبح مسؤوليته عليك: إذا رفعته على خدمة تخزين سحابي، تنطبق سياسة تلك الخدمة عليه.

1How you can check this yourself

This is not only a promise in a document: the app holds no internet permission at all, and an app without internet permission cannot open a connection.

Open your device's Settings → Apps → Elsakandary Study Planner → Permissions. You won't find internet or network access there.

2What is stored, and where

The app stores the following in its own private storage on your device, and nowhere else. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Your name, if you typed one during setup (optional), for the welcome message
  • Your subjects, their colours and weekly targets
  • Your schedule, session times and each session's goal
  • Focus sessions: when they started, how long they ran, your rating and notes
  • Exam dates and your weekly reviews
  • Your preferences: language, numerals, week start, reminder settings

3What does not happen

  • No accounts, no sign-in
  • No servers, no cloud sync
  • No ads, no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting
  • No third parties of any kind
  • No automatic Google Drive backup (deliberately disabled)

4Permissions, and why

NotificationsAn alarm, using your phone's alarm tone, when each session on your schedule starts; quiet reminders; a line of encouragement when you open the app; and the running session.
Exact alarmsSo the alarm rings at the minute it should. Decline it and the app still works, but the alarm may be a few minutes late.
Run at startupTo re-arm your alarms after a reboot, because Android clears alarms when the device restarts.
VibrateVibration with the session-start alarm.

You can decline any of them. The app keeps working; you simply lose that feature.

5Backups

You can export all of your data into a single file, only when you choose to. That file is:

  • Encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-derived key)
  • Saved wherever you choose, through the system file picker
  • Impossible to recover the password for. We hold no copy, and no server does

Once the file leaves the app it is in your hands: if you upload it to a cloud service, that service's policy applies to it.

6Deletion

The “Delete all data” button in Settings permanently erases everything from the device, and uninstalling does the same. There is nothing for us to delete, because we hold nothing.

7Age and children

The app is made for students aged 13 and over, and collects no data from any user of any age.

8Changes to this policy

Any change will appear here with its date. If anything material changes, in particular if the app ever gains internet permission or in-app purchases, it will be stated plainly and prominently.
